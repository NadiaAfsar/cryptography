# Cryptography Surveys

This repository contains two survey reports on cryptographic topics, written by **Nadia Afsar** at **Sharif University of Technology, Department of Mathematical Sciences**.

## Reports

### 1. A Survey on Foundational Concepts of Zero-Knowledge Proofs
A survey on the foundations and properties of ZKPs, covering formal definitions, classic examples, techniques for reducing soundness error, non-interactive constructions, and modern systems like STARKs.

### 2. A Survey on Cryptographic Voting Systems
A survey on cryptographic voting systems, covering mix-nets (Chaumian, re-encryption, Sako–Kilian, RPC, CCA2-based), Scratch & Vote, Helios, and return code schemes.
