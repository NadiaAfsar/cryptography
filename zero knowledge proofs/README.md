# A Survey on Foundational Concepts of Zero-Knowledge Proofs

**Author:** Nadia Afsar  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Affiliation:** Sharif University of Technology, Department of Mathematical Sciences

## Overview
This report surveys the foundations and properties of zero-knowledge proofs and explores several well-known examples. It also reviews techniques for improving their efficiency and reducing errors. Finally, it discusses a prominent application of ZKPs in modern digital systems, namely ZK-STARKs.
