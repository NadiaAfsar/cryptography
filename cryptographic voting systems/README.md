# A Survey on Cryptographic Voting Systems

**Author:** Nadia Afsar  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Affiliation:** Sharif University of Technology, Department of Mathematical Sciences

## Overview
This report surveys cryptographic voting systems and the reasons they are needed. It reviews techniques for improving efficiency and security. It also explores voting systems with mix-nets, discussing their advantages and disadvantages, and then examines voting systems without mix-nets.
